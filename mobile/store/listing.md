## Main store listing

**App name** (30 characters max): `Pace: Safe to Spend`

Pace is still a working name. Before you publish, check that nobody already holds the trademark and that no other app on Play uses the name.

**Short description** (80 characters max):

```
See what you can safely spend before payday, after bills and savings.
```

**Full description:**

```
Pace answers one question: how much can I spend without putting my next payday at risk?

Enter your pay, bills, balances and savings goals once. Pace works out a Safe to Spend amount until your next payday and shows exactly how it got there. That's your available cash, minus bills due before payday, planned savings, anything you've set aside and your safety buffer.

BUILT FOR STUDENTS AND YOUNG WORKERS
• Weekly, fortnightly or monthly pay, including shift work where pay changes
• Rent, phone, tuition, subscriptions and other bills, with due dates and reminders
• A safety buffer for surprises

SEE PROBLEMS BEFORE THEY HAPPEN
• A six-week cash forecast shows when bills could leave you short
• Simple fixes when a shortfall is coming: spend a little less each day, pause a goal, or review a bill

PLAN AND TRACK
• Budgets by category with planned, pending and completed spending
• Savings goals with estimated finish dates
• Insights: spending by category, month-to-month changes, and recurring bills

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your data stays on your phone. Pace has no account, no server and no ads.
• The bank connection in this version is a demonstration only. It uses made-up data and never contacts a real bank.

Pace is a budgeting tool, not financial advice. Safe to Spend is an estimate based on what you enter.
```

## App content answers

- **Privacy policy:** the URL from above.
- **Ads:** No, the app has no ads.
- **App access:** All functionality is available without special access. There is no login.
- **Content rating:** Complete the IARC questionnaire. Answer "No" to violence, sexual content, gambling, user-generated content and sharing location. Pace is a utility.
- **Target audience:** 18 and over. Choosing ages under 18 brings in the Families policy requirements.
- **News app:** No.
- **Data safety:** The app does **not collect or share** any user data. Everything is stored only on the device, and the app makes no network requests. Data isn't encrypted in transit because nothing is sent. Users can delete all data in the app (Settings → Delete all data).
- **Financial features declaration:** choose personal finance or budgeting management. Pace offers no loans, no payments, no trading and no access to bank accounts. The bank connection is simulated and labelled as a demo in the app.
- **Government app:** No.
