# Pace privacy policy

*Last updated: 22 October 2026*

Pace is a budgeting app. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter.

## What Pace stores

Pace stores the information you enter on your device, in the app's local storage. That includes your name if you give one, account balances, pay, bills, transactions, budgets, savings goals and any receipt photos you attach.

## What Pace sends

Nothing. Pace has no account system, no server, no analytics and no advertising. It doesn't send your information to us or to anyone else.

## Bank connections

This version of Pace doesn't connect to banks. You enter balances and transactions yourself. Pace never asks for, receives or stores your banking password.

## Camera and photos

If you attach a receipt, your phone asks you to choose a photo. That photo is stored only on your device, inside Pace.

## Deleting your data

To remove everything, go to Settings → Delete all data. Uninstalling Pace also removes all of its data from your device.

## Children

Pace is intended for people aged 18 and over.

## Changes

If a future version sends data anywhere, for example a real bank connection or cloud backup, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app will ask for your consent first.
